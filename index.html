Academic Research & Thesis Work

Title: Long-Term Impact of Immunization on Disease Prevention and Community Health

This research was conducted as a retrospective cross-sectional study involving 1,156 participants using WHO-validated datasets and structured questionnaires.

Methodology:
• Study Design: Retrospective Cross-Sectional Analysis
• Sample Size: 1,156 participants
• Tools: GraphPad Prism for statistical analysis
• Data Source: WHO-validated survey instruments

Key Findings:
• 97% immunization coverage observed among participants
• Significant reduction in preventable disease incidence in vaccinated populations
• Major barrier identified: accessibility constraints (69.71%)

Conclusion:
The study highlights the critical role of structured immunization programs in improving long-term public health outcomes and reducing preventable disease burden.
