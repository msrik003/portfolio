Research Project (Thesis Work)

Long-Term Impact of Immunization on Disease Prevention & Community Health

This research project was conducted as part of my PharmD academic thesis. It was designed as a retrospective cross-sectional study analyzing immunization coverage and its long-term effects on pediatric health outcomes.

✔ Study Population: 1,156 participants
✔ Data Source: WHO-validated questionnaires
✔ Methodology: Cross-sectional retrospective analysis
✔ Tool Used: GraphPad Prism for statistical evaluation

Key Findings:
• 97% infant immunization coverage rate observed
• Strong correlation between immunization compliance and reduced disease incidence
• Major barrier identified: logistical access issues (69.71%)

Outcome:
The study highlights the importance of strengthening immunization accessibility programs to improve long-term public health outcomes and reduce preventable diseases.
